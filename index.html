Frequently Asked Operations

Clear answers regarding our mobile field dispatches and lock engineering protocols.

Why are binding quotes only provided on-site?

Lock hardware specifications vary wildly depending on build age, internal security pins, and structural door wear. To protect consumers from pricing errors, a mobile specialist must physically audit the lock mechanism on location before generating a fixed invoice total.

Do you verify property ownership before unlocking doors?

Yes. To protect community safety and follow trade compliance rules, all technicians are legally required to verify valid government identification and supporting documents matching the vehicle registration or property address prior to initiating entry sequences.

Can you bypass modern smart electronic locks if the battery dies?

Absolutely. Our mobile fleet units carry secondary charging jump gear and override picks engineered specifically to interface with digital deadbolts safely without compromising the interior hardware component grids.

What is a master key system layout, and do you configure them?

Yes, we build commercial master key frameworks. A master key setup allows multi-family landlords or business managers to hold an overriding key that operates all doors, while individual tenant keys are pinned to unlock only their specific designated cylinders.

What happens if my original vehicle key breaks inside the door lock?

Do not attempt to force it out with wire. Our specialists use professional key extraction instruments to safely slide the broken metal piece along the internal pins without warping the cylinder walls, saving you from a full lock replacement.

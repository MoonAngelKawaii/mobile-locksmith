Frequently Asked Operations

Answers to standard operational questions regarding our site dispatches.

Why are binding quotes only provided on-site?

Lock hardware specifications vary wildly depending on build age, internal security pins, and structural wear. To protect consumers from pricing errors, a mobile specialist must physically audit the lock mechanism before generating a fixed invoice total.

Do you verify property ownership before unlocking doors?

Yes. To protect local property integrity and reduce liability risks, all technicians are legally required to verify valid government identification and supporting documents matching the vehicle or property address prior to entry.

Can you unlock modern smart locks if the battery dies?

Absolutely. Our mobile vans carry secondary charging jump gear and override picks engineered specifically to interface with electronic deadbolts without compromising the interior hardware.

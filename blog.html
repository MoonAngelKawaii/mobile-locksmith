Report #2401 • Residential

Understanding Lock Rekeying vs. Replacement

When modifying property access permissions due to tenant transitions or lost keys, full structural lock conversions are often an unnecessary hardware expense.

Professional rekeying targets only the internal brass pins of the lock cylinder. The physical lock shell stays intact on your door, but all previous key copies are instantly rendered useless. This cuts hardware waste and saves money while keeping security tight.

Keywords: Cylinder Rekeying, Pin Configurations
Report #2402 • Automotive

Laser-Cut Keys & Transponder Boards

Modern automotive security utilizes laser-cut "sidewinder" blades featuring continuous center tracks alongside internal RF transponder computer chips.

The cut mechanical blade only turns the ignition cylinder. To start the engine, the chip must send a verified code to the car's computer. Mobile fleet units interface directly with diagnostics ports on-site to safely configure these new digital systems.

Keywords: Immobilizer Frequencies, Calibration
Report #2403 • Commercial

Storefront Mortise Framework Maintenance

Commercial glass entryways rely on heavy-duty mortise locking bodies threaded directly inside structural pockets carved into aluminum door edges.

High daily use causes door hinges to sag over time, ruining alignment with the receiving frame strike plates. Regular field maintenance adjustments are highly critical to protect internal security parts and prevent massive unexpected lockouts.

Keywords: Mortise Hardware, Access Maintenance
